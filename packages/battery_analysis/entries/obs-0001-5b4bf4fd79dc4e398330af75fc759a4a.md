### 4. Functional flow around the batteries
**Charge side**
1. Produce Light (`85d456fe-c7d7-4f99-9899-a52ca133f10a`) sends Light to Convert Light to Power (`8365f92b-a3e8-49cb-b8ad-229c08f98b79`).
2. Convert Light to Power sends Power to Duplicate 1 (`9c68979f-7251-4163-a289-3dd3602e5080`) and Power to Charge Storage Device (`d14987fd-ac44-4ed9-96af-9c897efd4aab`), plus Convert Voltate to Gather 4 (`e46c1e27-7021-4367-b6a3-5df63591d5a0`).
3. Duplicate 1 sends Voltages to Convert Voltage Levels (`859cc793-7eed-445d-ab8f-2a309ff32d47`), Panel Voltage to Collect and Condition Signals, and Illuminate to Turn on Light Power.
4. Convert Voltage Levels sends BatterPower to Protect charge overcurrent (`40830d8e-59c5-4af2-8412-749f691d137a`), Charge Voltage to Gather 4, and Battery Charge Voltage to Collect and Condition Signals.
5. Charge Storage Device sends Charger Power to Protect charge overcurrent.
6. Protect charge overcurrent sends Chg Power (6395bb62) to Store Energy (`82d05564-34c7-4bb7-a93a-3900f3795104`) and Chg Power (25520ebc) to Store Energy (`fa435cae-7157-4394-9059-e25bc5801d3f`). The two Store Energy functions line up with the two cells.

**Discharge side**
1. Store Energy (82d05564) sends Store Power, and Store Energy (fa435cae) sends Bat Output, to Protect drain overcurrent (`1be17209-4729-426b-8dc9-a3a1202a2c61`).
2. Protect drain overcurrent sends Battery Power (7fad430d) to Distribute Bat Power (`21ffbf81-8b2a-43f8-abd9-4a4aee065f95`).
3. Distribute Bat Power sends Power (379ce405) and Boost Power (4aee3182) to Duplicate 3 (`5532b8d5-43ff-4e9a-808c-79768202ab14`), and Battery Voltage to Collect and Condition Signals.
4. Duplicate 3 sends Battery Power (c084ea6b) to Boost LI Voltage to USB (`aa876a39-b3fc-4e0e-86c5-aaa69f668e50`) and Battery Power (e43bdfaf) to Shut down for Low Voltage (`624e6a34-a5fa-4b6c-9ea7-d229d5b5f172`).
5. Boost LI Voltage to USB sends Energy to Accept Charger (`fac2a269-a677-48a1-9697-d78b84a82e5c`) and State to Split 18 (`527ffe0d-07fa-4383-93a4-b9d6c4005be8`). Split 18 feeds Turn on Light Power and Provide State.

**Telemetry of battery data:** Collect and Condition Signals (`170a9f02-b187-4be0-91f6-01dd8b6ab690`) takes in Battery Charge Voltage, Battery Voltage, Panel Voltage and Conversion Performance. It sends Signals to Sample Voltages, then Voltages to Send Telemetry Data, which outputs Performance (to Present Telemetry Data on Web Page) and Performance Information (to Gather Performance Information).

### 5. Observations and items to check
- **Cells:** two LI 18650 cells, each linked individually to the Fuse. Whether they are in series or parallel is not stated anywhere in what was returned.
- **No parameters:** no capacity, voltage, current limits or fuse rating came back. All element descriptions in the returned data were empty.
- **Flow direction vs. exchange orientation:** B-F1 and Bat 2 are defined cell to Fuse, but each also carries Chg Power, which flows toward the cells. C 7 is defined Fuse to Charger Indication Board, but also carries BatterPower, a charge-side flow. Check whether these exchanges are meant to be bidirectional; the orientation flag was not returned.
- **Two exchanges between the same function pair:** Distribute Bat Power sends both Power (379ce405) and Boost Power (4aee3182) to Duplicate 3. They may be redundant.
- **Dead end:** Shut down for Low Voltage has an input (Battery Power) and no outputs in the returned data, so nothing shows what it controls.
- **Protection:** Protect charge overcurrent and Protect drain overcurrent are separate functions. Which component they are allocated to was not returned.
- **Names:** BatterPower (typo), BAT-Fuese (typo), Duplicate 1 and Duplicate 3 (default names), "Bat 2␣" (stray space), "LI 18650␣" behavior components (stray space), and C 7 (also the name of another exchange).
